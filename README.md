# ClimbTime

> A pocket guide for your next vertical adventure.

**ClimbTime** is an Android application designed to help both beginners and experienced climbers discover new climbing destinations. Featuring a simple and intuitive interface, the app allows users to explore and plan outdoor or indoor activities with just a few taps.

---

## Features

* **Interactive Map:** Locate nearby climbing spots or search for new destinations based on your preferences.
* **Filter Options:** Filter by climbing type (Crags, Multipitches, Bouldering, Via Ferrata, Indoor Gyms).
* **Detailed Info:** View specific details for every location, including route difficulty, rock type, number of bolts, route length, style, anchor presence, and route status.
* **Save & Share:** Bookmark your favorite spots and easily share discovered locations with other climbers.
* **Navigation:** Launch direct navigation to your chosen destination.
* **User Profile:** Manage your account details and customize app themes.

---

## Tech Stack & Architecture

Built following modern Android native development practices:

* **Language & Framework:** Kotlin, Android SDK
* **Dependency Injection:** Koin
* **Architecture & Lifecycle:** ViewModel, Navigation Component, DataStore
* **Maps & Location Services:** Google Maps SDK, Play Services (GPS)
* **Backend & Auth:** Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage)
* **Image Loading:** Glide
* **Security:** Gradle Secrets Plugin
